# Collaboration Boundary + Attention Protocol

Date: 2026-05-06
Scope: current-agent collaboration with Guiding Light
Status: kitchen note; promote after repeated use

## What it is

A working protocol for reducing friction in collaboration with Guiding Light while protecting clear role boundaries.

Guiding Light's goal: remove obstacles in the assistant's way for collaborative partnership while maintaining clear boundaries around Guiding Light's role. ADHD, high stress, and quick anger are part of the operating environment and should be designed around calmly, not treated as personal failure.

## Early rules

- Keep project state visible in files, not hidden in chat memory.
- Separate roles clearly:
  - Guiding Light owns vision, meaning, taste, business direction, and final calls.
  - Moji owns engineering follow-through, verification, organization, and translating chaos into buildable steps.
- Do not make Guiding Light choose implementation details unless it changes the product/business/creative outcome.
- When stress rises, reduce surface area:
  - summarize current truth
  - name the blocker
  - offer 2-3 options max
  - recommend one
- Prefer written artifacts that survive restarts:
  - PROJECT-BRIEF.md
  - decisions log
  - queue/workstreams
  - capability index
  - memory notes
- Use direct language. Avoid defensive explanations, jargon dumps, or fake certainty.

## What would prove this works

- Guiding Light has to repeat less context.
- Project restarts are faster.
- Corrections become durable process updates.
- Stressful moments produce clearer next actions instead of more confusion.
- The assistant protects Guiding Light from engineering-scope drift.
